# Vendor Management

Any vendor processing company or client data requires a data processing
agreement signed before data is shared. A trial or proof of concept with real
data is not an exception.

Vendors are risk-assessed before onboarding. The assessment covers where data is
stored, who can access it, the vendor's own subprocessors, and how the
relationship can be exited.

Vendor support tickets are answered within four business hours for priority one
and within two business days for priority three, where the contract provides for
it. Escalation to a named account manager is available for priority one.

Vendor access to internal systems follows the same access control rules as
employees, including time-boxed access to restricted data and removal on
contract end.

Contracts are reviewed annually. A vendor holding restricted data is reassessed
annually regardless of contract length.

Exiting a vendor requires written confirmation that company data has been
deleted from their systems and from their backups, with a deletion date.
