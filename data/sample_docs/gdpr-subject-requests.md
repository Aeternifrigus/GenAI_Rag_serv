# Data Subject Requests

A data subject request is any request from an individual to access, correct,
export or delete the personal data held about them. Requests may arrive through
any channel, including a reply to a marketing email, and must be forwarded to
the data protection officer on the day they are received.

The statutory response deadline is one calendar month from receipt. This may be
extended by two further months for complex requests, but only if the subject is
told of the extension inside the original month.

Access requests are answered with a machine-readable export of the personal data
plus a description of the purposes it is processed for and who it is shared
with.

Deletion requests are actioned within thirty days where no legal retention
obligation applies. Where an obligation applies, the data is restricted from all
processing instead of deleted, and the subject is told which obligation prevents
deletion.

Requests are logged whether or not they are granted. Refusing a request without
recording the reason is itself a compliance failure.
