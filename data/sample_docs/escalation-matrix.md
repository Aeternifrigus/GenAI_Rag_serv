# Escalation Matrix

Escalation moves an incident to someone with more authority, more context or
more availability. It is not a signal of failure and carries no penalty.

For severity one, the incident commander notifies the engineering lead
immediately and the executive on duty within thirty minutes. Customer
communications are drafted by the communications team and approved by the
incident commander before publication.

For severity two, the engineering lead is notified within one hour. No executive
notification is required unless the incident crosses four hours.

For severity three, no escalation is required. The owning team handles it during
working hours.

Any responder may escalate at any time without seeking permission. A responder
who is stuck for thirty minutes is expected to escalate rather than continue
alone.

Where an incident involves suspected data exposure, legal and the data
protection officer are notified in parallel with the technical escalation,
regardless of assigned severity, and the clock for regulatory notification
starts at that moment.
