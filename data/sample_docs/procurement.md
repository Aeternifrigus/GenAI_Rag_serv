# Procurement

Software and services below 500 EUR annually may be purchased on a corporate
card by the requesting team, provided no company or client data will be sent to
the supplier.

Purchases above 500 EUR annually require a purchase order raised in advance.
Splitting a purchase to stay below the threshold is a policy breach.

Anything that will process company or client data requires a vendor risk
assessment and a signed data processing agreement before purchase, regardless of
price, including free tools.

Renewals are treated as new purchases at the same thresholds. An auto-renewing
contract above 500 EUR must be flagged ninety days before renewal so the
decision is deliberate.

Procurement approval confirms the commercial and compliance position. It does
not confirm technical suitability, which remains with the requesting team.

Invoices are matched against the purchase order before payment. An invoice with
no matching order is held and returned to the requester rather than paid.
