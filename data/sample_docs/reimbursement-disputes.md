# Reimbursement Disputes

A claim rejected by finance is returned to the claimant with a written reason.
The claimant has ten working days to supply the missing information or to open a
dispute. Claims left unanswered after ten working days are closed and cannot be
reopened.

Disputes are reviewed by the finance lead, who must respond within fifteen
working days. Where the finance lead upholds the rejection, the claimant may
escalate once to the finance director, whose decision is final.

A claim rejected only for a missing receipt may be resubmitted with a signed
declaration of expenditure where the receipt cannot be recovered. Declarations
are accepted at most twice per employee per financial year.

Disputes concerning amounts below 20 EUR are settled in the claimant's favour
without review, because the cost of reviewing them exceeds the amount in
question.

Rejected claims are retained in the expense system for seven years alongside
approved ones, since both form part of the financial record.
