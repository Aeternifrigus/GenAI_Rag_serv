# On-Call Rotation

The primary on-call rotation runs weekly, handing over at 10:00 local time on
Wednesday. A secondary is always rostered and is paged only when the primary
does not acknowledge inside the response window.

Nobody is rostered primary for more than one week in any four-week period. An
engineer may swap a shift with any other engineer on the same rotation by
recording the swap in the roster before the shift begins.

On-call engineers must be reachable, sober and within thirty minutes of a
working internet connection for the duration of the shift. They are not expected
to be at a keyboard continuously.

Compensation is paid per shift, not per page. A shift containing no pages is
compensated identically to a shift containing many.

An engineer paged between 23:00 and 07:00 for more than one hour is entitled to
a late start the following working day, arranged directly with their manager and
requiring no approval.

New joiners shadow two full rotations before being rostered as primary.
