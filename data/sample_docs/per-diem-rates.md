# Per Diem Rates

The daily allowance covers meals and incidental costs while travelling on
business. It is paid instead of itemised meal receipts, not in addition to them.
An employee claiming per diem for a day may not also claim individual meal
expenses for that day.

Rates are 65 EUR per day for international travel, 45 EUR per day for domestic
travel, and 30 EUR per day where accommodation includes an evening meal.

Partial days are paid at half rate when travel begins after midday or ends
before midday. A day with no overnight stay attracts no per diem regardless of
hours travelled.

Where a client or conference host provides meals, the per diem is reduced by
15 EUR for lunch and 25 EUR for dinner. The traveller is responsible for
declaring provided meals on the claim.

Per diem is not payable for days spent at a permanent company office, including
offices in other countries. Those days fall under the normal expenses policy.
