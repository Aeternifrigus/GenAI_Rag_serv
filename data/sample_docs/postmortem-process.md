# Postmortem Process

A written postmortem is mandatory for every severity one and severity two
incident. Severity three postmortems are optional and written at the incident
commander's discretion.

The postmortem is owned by the incident commander and must be circulated in
draft within five working days of resolution, and finalised within ten.

Postmortems are blameless. They describe what the system allowed to happen, not
who typed what. Naming individuals as causes is grounds for the document being
sent back for rewriting.

Every postmortem records a timeline, the customer impact in concrete terms, the
contributing factors, and the action items. Action items must have a named owner
and a due date, or they are not action items.

Action items from severity one incidents are tracked to completion by the
engineering lead and reviewed monthly until closed. Action items from severity
two incidents enter the normal backlog.

Postmortems are readable by everyone in the company. They are not shared
externally without approval from legal and communications.
