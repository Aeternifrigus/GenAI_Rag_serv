# Corporate Card Usage

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times per
year, on approval from their line manager and the finance team. The card remains
the property of the company and must be returned on the last working day.

The card may be used for travel, accommodation, client entertainment and
software purchases below 500 EUR. It may not be used for personal purchases of
any amount, even where the employee intends to repay the company later. Personal
use is treated as a policy breach rather than a reimbursement question.

Every card transaction must be reconciled in the expense system within fourteen
days of the statement date. Transactions unreconciled after thirty days trigger
an automatic card suspension until the backlog is cleared.

Lost or stolen cards must be reported to the card provider immediately and to
finance within one working day. The employee is not liable for fraudulent
transactions reported inside that window.

Cash advances on the corporate card are disabled by default and are enabled only
for travel to countries where card acceptance is unreliable.
