# Travel Booking Policy

All business travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel portal.
Bookings made outside the portal require written approval from a department head
before the trip, and approval granted after travel has taken place is not valid
for reimbursement.

Economy class is the standard for all flights under six hours of scheduled air
time. Premium economy is permitted on flights above six hours. Business class
requires director-level approval regardless of flight duration and is never
approved for domestic travel.

Hotel bookings are capped at 180 EUR per night in tier one cities and 120 EUR
per night elsewhere. The portal enforces these caps automatically. Where no
compliant room is available, the traveller may book above the cap and must
attach the portal's unavailability confirmation to the claim.

Rail travel is preferred over air travel for journeys under four hours
city centre to city centre. First class rail is permitted where the fare is
below the equivalent economy air fare.

Travel must be booked at least fourteen days in advance. Bookings inside
fourteen days require a written justification recorded in the portal.
