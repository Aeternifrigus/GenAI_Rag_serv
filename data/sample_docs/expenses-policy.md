# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may claim reimbursement for business expenses incurred while travelling
on company business. All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense
being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days require written approval from a
department head.

Receipts are mandatory for any single expense above 25 EUR. Expenses below that
threshold may be claimed without a receipt but must still be itemised.

Standard daily allowance for international travel is 65 EUR, covering meals and
incidental costs. Accommodation is reimbursed at actual cost up to 180 EUR per
night in major cities and 120 EUR elsewhere.

Air travel must be booked in economy class for flights under six hours. Business
class is permitted only for flights exceeding six hours and requires approval in
advance from a line manager.
