# Data Retention Standard

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was
collected. Retention periods are defined per data category and reviewed annually.

Client transaction records are retained for seven years to satisfy regulatory
obligations. Marketing contact data is retained for two years from the last
recorded interaction, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Application logs containing personal identifiers are retained for 90 days.
Aggregated logs with identifiers stripped may be retained indefinitely for
capacity planning.

Deletion requests from data subjects are actioned within 30 calendar days.
Where a legal obligation prevents deletion, the requester is informed of the
specific basis for retention.

Backups are exempt from immediate deletion but roll off on their own 35 day cycle.
